Per-tool "Instructions" for the AI agent (AI agent designer > AI agents > your agent > Tools > edit tool > Instructions).
Paste the text under each heading into the matching tool.

[list_branch_cities]
Use when a city has no branch, or when the customer asks which cities have a branch. Read the returned city names as a short spoken list.

[get_branch_status]
Use for open or closed right now, opening hours, address and services. Requires a city. Report local_time, open_now and reason exactly as returned. If found is false, call list_branch_cities. If a branch status is not ACTIVE, give the reason text.

[get_branch_representatives]
Use only when the customer asks how many staff or representatives work at a branch. Requires a city. Report tellers, customer service and loan officers per branch, and total_representatives.

[list_loan_products]
Use to get current interest rates, limits and tenure ranges. Call it before calculate_emi when the customer did not give an interest rate.

[check_loan_eligibility]
Requires product_type (personal, car or home) and monthly_salary (monthly, never annual). Pass existing_monthly_debt as 0 if the customer has none. Report eligible, reasons and indicative_max_amount, then always read the disclaimer.

[calculate_emi]
Requires principal, annual_interest_rate and tenure_months. Report monthly_payment, total_repayment and total_interest in words.

[Escalate]
Use when the customer asks for a person, wants to complain, reports fraud, a scam, or a lost or stolen card, needs details of their own account, or is still stuck after troubleshooting. Tell the customer you are connecting them to a representative before using this tool.

[Complete]
Use only after the customer confirms they have no further questions. Thank the customer first.
