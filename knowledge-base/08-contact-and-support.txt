HORIZON BANK - CONTACT AND SUPPORT
Document type: Customer FAQ
Note: Horizon Bank is a fictional bank used for training purposes only.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT HOURS

- Phone and live chat: Monday to Friday 08:00 to 20:00, Saturday 09:00 to 14:00 Eastern Time.
- Lost or stolen cards and fraud: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Virtual assistant: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

WHAT THE VIRTUAL ASSISTANT CAN HELP WITH

- Mobile app and online banking troubleshooting.
- Information about accounts, products and fees.
- Branch opening status and the number of representatives at a branch.
- Indicative loan eligibility checks and repayment estimates.
- Security and fraud awareness tips.

WHEN YOU WILL BE TRANSFERRED TO A PERSON

The virtual assistant transfers you to a human representative when:
- You ask to speak to a person.
- You want to make a complaint.
- You report fraud, a scam, or a lost or stolen card.
- Your question needs access to your personal account details.
- The assistant cannot solve your problem after troubleshooting.

MAKING A COMPLAINT

We aim to acknowledge complaints within one business day and resolve them within 15 business days. You can complain by phone, in a branch, or in the Horizon Mobile app under Help, Make a Complaint.

AVERAGE WAITING TIMES

- Phone, weekday mornings: under 5 minutes.
- Phone, weekday evenings and Saturdays: 10 to 15 minutes.
- Live chat: under 3 minutes.
Waiting times are longer on the first and last business day of each month.
