HORIZON BANK - FEES AND CHARGES SCHEDULE
Document type: Product information
Note: Horizon Bank is a fictional bank used for training purposes only. All figures are dummy values.

ACCOUNT FEES
- Everyday Checking monthly maintenance fee: 8 USD (waived with 1,500 USD monthly direct deposits or a 2,000 USD minimum daily balance).
- Student Checking monthly maintenance fee: none.
- Paper statements: 2 USD per statement. Electronic statements are free.
- Account closure within 90 days of opening: 25 USD.

ATM AND CASH FEES
- Horizon Bank ATMs: free.
- Other banks' ATMs in the country: 2.50 USD per withdrawal, plus any fee charged by the ATM owner.
- ATM withdrawals abroad: 5 USD plus 3 percent of the amount.
- Cash withdrawal at a branch counter: free.

TRANSFER FEES
- Transfers between your own Horizon Bank accounts: free.
- Domestic transfers to other banks: free for standard transfers (1 to 2 business days), 15 USD for same-day wire transfers.
- International wire transfers: 35 USD outgoing, 15 USD incoming.

CARD FEES
- Replacement debit card (standard delivery): free once per year, then 5 USD.
- Express card delivery: 20 USD.
- Foreign transaction fee on card purchases: 3 percent.

OVERDRAFT AND RETURNED PAYMENTS
- Overdraft fee: 30 USD per item, maximum three fees per day.
- Returned payment (insufficient funds): 30 USD.
- Overdraft fees are not charged if the overdraft is less than 50 USD at the end of the business day.

LOAN FEES
- Personal loan arrangement fee: 1 percent of the loan amount, minimum 100 USD.
- Late loan payment: 25 USD or 5 percent of the missed payment, whichever is lower.
- Early repayment of a personal or car loan: no fee.
- Early repayment of a home loan in the first three years: 1 percent of the amount repaid early.

HOW TO AVOID FEES
- Switch to electronic statements.
- Use Horizon Bank ATMs. The Horizon Mobile app has an ATM map.
- Set up low-balance alerts in the app to avoid overdraft fees.
