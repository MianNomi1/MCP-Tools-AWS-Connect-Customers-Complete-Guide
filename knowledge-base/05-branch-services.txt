HORIZON BANK - BRANCH SERVICES GUIDE
Document type: Customer FAQ
Note: Horizon Bank is a fictional bank used for training purposes only.

WHAT YOU CAN DO AT A BRANCH

Every Horizon Bank branch offers:
- Cash deposits and withdrawals at the counter.
- Cheque deposits.
- Opening joint accounts and business accounts.
- Updating your phone number, address or email address (photo ID required).
- Replacing a lost or damaged debit card. A temporary card can be printed on the spot at branches marked "Instant Card".
- Meeting a loan officer to discuss personal, car or home loans.
- Help with the Horizon Mobile app and online banking.

Some services are only available at larger branches:
- Safe deposit boxes.
- Foreign currency exchange. Currency must be ordered two business days in advance at smaller branches.
- Mortgage specialists (home loans). Smaller branches can book a video appointment with a mortgage specialist instead.

OPENING HOURS

Most branches are open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 local time. Some branches open on Saturdays. Branches are closed on Sundays and on public holidays. Opening hours can change, and branches can close temporarily for renovation. The virtual assistant checks live branch status and hours from the branch system, so always ask the assistant or check the app before visiting.

STAFF AT BRANCHES

Branches have three types of representatives:
- Tellers handle cash, cheques and everyday transactions.
- Customer service representatives help with accounts, cards, app support and general questions.
- Loan officers help with loan applications and eligibility.
The number of representatives differs by branch. The virtual assistant can tell you how many representatives of each type work at a branch.

BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT

You do not need an appointment for counter services. For loan meetings and mortgage advice, book an appointment in the Horizon Mobile app under Branches, Book Appointment, so a loan officer is available when you arrive.

WHAT TO BRING

- A government-issued photo ID for any account change.
- Your debit card if you want to withdraw cash at the counter.
- For loan meetings: recent payslips and bank statements.

ACCESSIBILITY

All branches have step-free access. Hearing loops are available at the counter. If you need extra help, ask any representative or tell us when you book an appointment.
